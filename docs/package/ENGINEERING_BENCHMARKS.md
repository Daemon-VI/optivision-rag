## Method

- **Time.** `time.perf_counter`; best and median of N repeats of one call after one untimed warm-up call (N is stated in each section). A call under about 20 ms is looped inside a repeat and reported per call; `gc.collect()` runs before each repeat. Comparisons run in rounds, every variant once per round with a rotating start (sections 2, 4 and 7); the shares in sections 1, 3 and 5 compare calls timed one after the other and carry the drift of the run.
- **Memory.** Peak of `tracemalloc` during the warm-up call (Python and NumPy allocations, not BLAS-internal buffers), and array `nbytes`.
- **Data.** `optivision.testing.make_corpus` and `make_queries`, seeds 20261006 and 20261007: 30 to 60 unit-norm Gaussian vectors per document, 4 to 8 per query cut from a labelled document with noise 2.5. No model output and no research store is read.
- **Scope.** One process, default BLAS threading, CPU only; every call is given a whole query set (the NumPy index scores its queries one at a time inside the call). Nothing here evaluates retrieval quality.

## What these numbers do not show

- other hardware, BLAS builds, thread counts, or Python and NumPy versions; this laptop's two CPU speed states mean that even on this machine absolute times differ between runs
- real corpora or real encoder output: the vectors are Gaussian, so merging, codecs and selection behave as on random data, and a selection outcome here is an output of the code, not a finding
- larger scale: sizes stop at a few hundred documents, and the dense score matrix and the decoded blocks grow with the corpus; nothing here is a statement about large corpora
- memory of `certify()` on a large corpus: section 7 gives one traced peak at one small size; the transform cache alone can hold several whole transformed corpora until its cap, beyond which it evicts and recomputes (slower, same results), and that regime was not measured
- a GPU or `OPTIVISION_SCORE_DEVICE` (all scoring ran on the CPU through NumPy)
- single-query latency: no timing here is of one query alone
- concurrency, memory outside tracemalloc's view, or disk speed beyond one temporary directory
- retrieval quality, agreement with the uncompressed ranking, or anything about an encoder
- any relation to the Q4, Q5/Q6 or Q8 measurements or to the registered experiments
